# Household Water Efficiency & Benchmarks

## Overview
Water efficiency in residential settings involves taking practical steps to reduce unneeded consumption, eliminate waste, and optimize water reuse. Understanding household benchmarks allows families and individuals to evaluate their daily water footprint against sustainable targets.

## SDG 6 Alignment
UN Sustainable Development Goal 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation) aims to ensure availability and sustainable management of water for all by 2030. Target 6.4 specifically calls for substantially increasing water-use efficiency across all sectors and ensuring sustainable withdrawals to address water scarcity.

## Daily Household Consumption Benchmarks
- **Global Average Per Capita Baseline**: 150 - 250 liters per person per day for residential settings.
- **Efficient Water-Steward Baseline**: 100 - 130 liters per person per day.
- **High-Stress Region Target**: Under 100 liters per person per day without sacrificing health or sanitation.

## Major Household Water Consumption Categories
1. **Bathing & Showers**: 30% - 35% of indoor water use.
2. **Toilet Flushing**: 24% - 30% of indoor water use.
3. **Laundry & Washing**: 18% - 22% of indoor water use.
4. **Kitchen & Cooking**: 8% - 12% of indoor water use.
5. **Outdoor & Gardening**: 15% - 40% (varies significantly by season and yard size).

## Strategic Approach to Conservation
To achieve long-term sustainability:
- Measure daily usage systematically to establish baseline trends.
- Focus initial conservation efforts on the single highest-consuming activity.
- Combine hardware upgrades (aerators, low-flow fixtures) with daily habit shifts.
