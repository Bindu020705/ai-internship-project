# Gardening and Outdoor Water Efficiency

## Outdoor Water Management
Outdoor water usage often spikes significantly during dry and summer months, accounting for up to 40% of total residential consumption.

## Smart Irrigation Techniques
- **Drip Irrigation Systems**: Delivers water directly to plant roots with 90%+ efficiency, minimizing evaporation compared to overhead sprinklers (50-60% efficiency).
- **Watering Schedule**: Irrigate gardens early in the morning (before 8:00 AM) or late in the evening to drastically reduce surface evaporation caused by midday sun and wind.
- **Mulching**: Applying a 5-7 cm layer of organic mulch around plant bases retains soil moisture, suppresses weeds, and reduces watering frequency by up to 30%.

## Native Plants & Xeriscaping
- **Drought-Tolerant Species**: Replacing thirsty lawn turf grass with drought-tolerant native plants, succulents, and perennials reduces landscape water requirements by 50% to 75%.
- **Hydrozoning**: Group plants together according to their specific water needs to avoid over-watering low-water plants.

## Rainwater Harvesting & Greywater Reuse
- **Rain Barrels**: Collecting roof runoff in rain barrels provides non-potable water for garden beds and potted plants. A typical 200-liter barrel fills quickly during moderate rainfall.
- **Laundry-to-Landscape Greywater**: Reusing rinse water from clothes washing or veggie rinsing for non-edible outdoor plants reuses clean water effectively.
