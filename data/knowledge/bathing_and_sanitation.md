# Bathing and Sanitation Water Efficiency

## Water Savings in the Bathroom
Bathroom activities represent over 50% of total indoor residential water consumption. Implementing high-efficiency fixtures and behavioral adjustments yields immediate savings.

## Efficient Showering Guidelines
- **Standard Showerhead Flow Rate**: 12 to 15 liters per minute (LPM).
- **Eco Low-Flow Aerated Showerhead**: 6 to 8 LPM (saves up to 50% water while maintaining spray pressure).
- **Shower Duration Target**: Reducing shower length from 10 minutes to 5 minutes saves up to 40 - 60 liters per shower.
- **Navy Shower Technique**: Turning off the water while lathering shampoo/soap reduces consumption by up to 65%.

## Toilet Flush Optimization
- **Older Single-Flush Toilets**: 13 to 18 liters per flush.
- **Modern Dual-Flush Toilets**: 3 liters for liquid waste, 6 liters for solid waste.
- **Retrofit Solutions**: Dual-flush conversion valves or displacement bags in older cisterns save 2 to 4 liters per flush.
- **Never Use Toilets as Trash Cans**: Flushing tissues or small debris wastes 6-12 liters of clean water unnecessarily.

## Sink Aerators & Faucet Habits
- **Standard Faucet Flow**: 10 to 12 LPM.
- **Low-Flow Faucet Aerator**: 2 to 3.8 LPM.
- **Turning Off Tap While Brushing**: Shutting the tap off while shaving or brushing teeth saves approximately 12 liters per instance.
