# Laundry and Kitchen Cleaning Water Efficiency

## Efficient Laundry Practices
Washing machines account for roughly 20% of residential indoor water use. Equipment selection and load management play crucial roles in overall efficiency.

## Front-Load vs Top-Load Washing Machines
- **Traditional Top-Loader**: Uses 110 to 150 liters per full load.
- **High-Efficiency (HE) Front-Loader**: Uses 40 to 60 liters per load (up to 60% savings).
- **Full Load Rule**: Running washing machines only with full loads saves over 3,000 liters annually compared to multiple half-full loads.
- **Eco Cycles**: Utilizing cold water eco-cycles minimizes energy while conserving wash water duration.

## Kitchen & Dishwashing Optimization
- **Hand Washing vs Automatic Dishwasher**: An Energy Star certified dishwasher uses 10 to 14 liters per cycle, whereas hand-washing dish loads under running taps can consume 40 to 80 liters.
- **Dual-Basin Soaking**: If hand-washing dishes, fill one basin with soapy wash water and one with rinse water rather than leaving water running continuously.
- **Scraping Over Rinsing**: Scrape food scraps off plates into compost/trash rather than rinsing plates under running faucets prior to loading dishwashers.
