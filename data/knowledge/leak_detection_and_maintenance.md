# Leak Detection and System Maintenance

## Detecting Household Water Leaks
Unnoticed household leaks waste thousands of liters per month and are a leading cause of sudden spikes in daily water consumption metrics.

## Common Leak Indicators
- **Silent Toilet Flapper Leak**: A worn flapper valve can leak 200 to 700 liters per day silently into the bowl. Test by adding 4-5 drops of food dye into the toilet tank; if color appears in the bowl after 15 minutes without flushing, the flapper valve needs replacement.
- **Dripping Faucets**: A faucet dripping at 1 drop per second wastes over 11,000 liters of water annually.
- **Unusual Consumption Spikes**: If consumption on a specific date increases by 100-300% without changes in household occupancy or activities, inspect outdoor taps, pressure relief valves, and main line connections.

## Water Meter Over-Night Test
1. Turn off all water outlets, faucets, and appliances (washing machine, dishwasher, ice maker).
2. Record the exact meter reading or take a photo before going to sleep.
3. Check the meter reading in the morning before any water is used. If the reading has changed, an undetected leak is present in the plumbing network.
